Track, Measure, and Grow
All on One Platform

Our Mobile App
Designed for on-ground tech teams and farmers, the mobile app simplifies data collection with GPS auto-location, sensor integration, and step-by-step measurement flows. Farmers can easily view summaries of their soil health and carbon contribution in a simple, visual format.


Our Dashboard
Built for researchers, agronomists, and funding partners, the dashboard offers a full bird’s-eye view of all farm activities. Access live carbon data, view geospatial farm maps, download reports, and monitor farmer progress in real time—all from a central, intuitive interface.


Together, the dashboard and app enable transparency at scale, ensuring every stakeholder—from funder to farmer—stays connected, informed, and empowered.
